How to use this page

  1. Pharmacology → Drug Overview: the detailed view, one card per drug. Read the one-line mechanism, then open the card for mechanism, clinical uses, adverse effects, contraindications and monitoring, pharmacokinetics, HY notes, traps and buzzwords. Answer the four recall checks out loud before you reveal them.
  2. Pharmacology → Drug Table: the condensed high-yield chart, one row per drug. Click a drug name to jump to its card. Turn on Cover names or cover a column and rebuild it from memory.
  3. DDX: the cross-cutting high-yield patterns (one toggle per pattern) and side-by-side comparison tables of drugs that are easy to confuse.
  4. Practice Qs → Practice DDX: 37 rapid-fire clue sets. Name the drug in under 10 seconds.
  5. Practice Qs → Practice MCQs: 38 Step 1–style vignettes with the explanation and the trap. Aim for 90% before the exam.
  6. The Drugs list, search, drug-class menus and Exam topics filter both Pharmacology views at once.
